Good coffee. Good food. A place to feel at home.

Maya Cafe is a neighborhood cafe in Kombolcha, Ethiopia. We bring people together over freshly brewed coffee, satisfying meals, and the small comforts that make a visit feel special.

Rooted in coffee culture

Coffee is more than a drink here. We celebrate the Ethiopian coffee tradition with carefully prepared coffee alongside espresso favorites, refreshing juices, and a menu made for sharing.

Made for your everyday

Stop in for breakfast, meet a friend for lunch, or take your favorite drink to go. We look forward to welcoming you.
